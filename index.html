Erie Regional Chamber and Growth Partnership

Signed · starts week of Sept 28
$1,500 · 7 weeks · 1 student

Phase 1 of the Chamber's Erie 300 place-brand work: a fully sourced evidence base on how the Erie region actually performs, how it is perceived from outside, and one to three positioning themes the data genuinely supports. Built from public and published data only; message testing with real audiences is a designed later phase, not a gap.

Client
Brandon Mendoza, President and CEO. Introduced by Chancellor Ralph Ford.
Student team
Davis Minder, marketing, leading the strategy and client work. Faculty covering the data layer directly, with ERIE on regional economics.
Faculty
Kyle Chalupczynski, advising. Kevin Pratt on positioning from mid‑October. The Economic Research Institute of Erie, directed by Dr. Ken Louie, confirmed on the regional economic data with research assistant support.
Research method
Regional and peer-metro figures pull directly from Census, BLS, and IRS, so every number carries its source, geography, and year. Built in-house and reusable on future projects.
Delivers
Final presentation to the Chamber the week of November 16, 2026.

What one engagement becomes

Plastikos began as a single eight-week, $2,000 project. Because the deliverables were working AI tools rather than a report, the work grew on its own: a second department asked for its own assistant, then a sales-intelligence system. That expansion pattern is the program's core argument.
